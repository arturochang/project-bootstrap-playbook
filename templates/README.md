# Templates

- [`product-plan.md`](product-plan.md) — idea, users, workflows, requirements,
  scope, data, security, architecture, provider posture, operations, cost, risks,
  delivery target, and approval.
- [`setup-provenance.md`](setup-provenance.md) — immutable playbook source plus
  the repository-specific commands, artifact mapping, assumptions, evidence, and
  remote actions produced during setup.

Templates are starting structures. Remove inapplicable rows and combine small
documents when clearer, while preserving the required decisions and evidence.
